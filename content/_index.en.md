I build and maintain tools for how software gets built, tested and shipped: open source like [cpp-linter](https://github.com/cpp-linter) and [commit-check](https://github.com/commit-check/commit-check), and products like [keelinfra](https://keelinfra.io) and [keelapps](https://keelapps.app). If one fits your setup, use it.
